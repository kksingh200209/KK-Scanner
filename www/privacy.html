Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

The short version

KK keeps your scans on your own phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never uploads your documents anywhere. We cannot see them.

What KK stores, and where

  • Your scans, folders and settings are stored in your browser's or phone's own storage (IndexedDB on the web version, the app's private storage on Android). They stay on your device.
  • App lock, if you turn it on, stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of your PIN on the device. The web version's lock keeps other people out of the app; it does not encrypt the stored files.
  • Backups are a single file you create and keep wherever you choose. KK does not send it anywhere.

Camera and photos

KK asks for the camera only when you scan. Pictures are processed on your phone. If you choose photos from your gallery, KK reads only the ones you pick.

What we collect

Nothing. KK does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it has no advertising or tracking code. The Android app does not request the internet permission.

If you open the web version from a hosting service, that service (for example Netlify) may keep ordinary server logs such as your IP address and the time of the visit, as every website does. Those logs are the host's, not KK's, and they never contain your scans.

Sharing

A scan leaves your phone only when you share or save it yourself, for example with Share PDF or Save a copy. After that, the app you chose to send it to is responsible for it. A PDF password protects the file you export; if you forget it, KK cannot recover it.

Sensitive documents

You may scan ID cards such as Aadhaar or PAN. They are kept only on your device, and you can use the Hide tool to cover numbers before sharing. KK is not affiliated with UIDAI, the Income Tax Department or any government body.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete everything removes all scans and folders from the device. Deleting a scan removes it for good. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser also removes everything. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Children

KK is a general tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
